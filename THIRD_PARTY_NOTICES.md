# Bundled dependency notices

This repository includes the following browser libraries and their license files. The GoodConvertPDF license does not replace these licenses.

- pdf-lib: `extension/vendor/licenses/pdf-lib-LICENSE.md`
- fflate: `extension/vendor/licenses/fflate-LICENSE`
- DOMPurify: `extension/vendor/licenses/dompurify-LICENSE` and `dompurify-LICENSE-MPL`
- Turndown and its GFM plugin: `extension/vendor/licenses/turndown-LICENSE` and `turndown-plugin-gfm-LICENSE`
- Mozilla Readability: `extension/vendor/licenses/readability-LICENSE.md`
- PDF.js: `extension/vendor/licenses/pdfjs-dist-LICENSE`
- html2pdf.js 0.10.2 bundle (including its bundled dependencies): `extension/vendor/licenses/html2pdf.bundle.min.js.LICENSE.txt`

Before a public release, review the provenance and license of every remaining bundled asset, including icons. The exporter verifies that the named license files are present but cannot establish ownership of every asset.
